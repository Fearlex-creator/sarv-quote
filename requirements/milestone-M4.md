## Authoritative typed command and replay contract

`ProcurementCommand` has exactly `Resources(RegisterResources)`, `Order(PurchaseOrder)`, `Receive(ReceiveOrder)`, `Bill(MatchBill)` and `Transfer(TransferStock)`. The public entry points are `preview_procurement`, `post_procurement_command`, `procurement_resources`, `procurement_document` and `procurement_reconcile(company_id, as_of)`. These names define the integration boundary; final evidence must identify the actual completed calls and immutable source, rather than treating this contract as a passing run.

Every `ProcurementContext` carries company/period/setup, source ID/revision, ISO financial date, request key, actor and reason. A posted record retains the complete command digest, frozen setup, company sequence, data, stock movement before/after snapshots and `journal_id: Option<String>`. Record ID is distinct from journal ID. `ProcurementRecorded` is the nonfinancial audit; financial effects retain the normal `JournalPosted` boundary. A positive-cost transfer still has `journal_id: None`; zero-amount operational effects also use the nonfinancial path. No empty journal or manufactured one-rial amount is allowed.

Resources append validated parties/units/items/warehouses to opening-seeded snapshots without editing the immutable opening. Orders freeze supplier, item/unit ratios, source price reference/date/override reason, gross/line and document-discount allocation/net. Receipt quantities convert using the frozen order factors, then consume original PO cost cumulatively; final receipt consumes the exact residual. Bill quantities are **base-unit thousandths**, reference an operational receipt line or opening GRNI ID, and must match each frozen cumulative receipt allocation. Opening GRNI is billable only from the opening availability boundary; it creates neither historical receipt nor another stock movement.

`ProcurementRequest` retains full business hash excluding retry key for every financial/nonfinancial alias. Procurement deliberately uses stronger natural `(company, source_id, event)` ownership: one posted effect across all source revisions. The generic manual ledger business identity remains `(company, source_id, source_revision, event)`; this milestone does not claim all ledger source identities are revision-independent or provide an arbitrary revised-source correction workflow. Linked correction requirements remain separately tracked; verified legacy histories are not silently rewritten. Exact same/different-key commands resolve the same record; changed quantity/due/provenance-only content conflicts even with unchanged GL. The global namespace rejects collisions with ordinary and M3 zero requests, allowing only the validated paired normal-financial/typed representation of that one effect. Replay re-executes complete commands and aliases, restores historical record/journal/audit identities and order, validates immutable prefixes and compares complete state. Store adds durable generation CAS to clone-and-validate atomic command behavior.

Historical manual namespace is preserved: actual M3 journals named `procurement.receive` or `procurement.receive.v1` remain ordinary manual history with exact requests/aliases/audit and linked reversals. Typed protection/source navigation follows an actual immutable core/procurement association, not an event-text prefix. Existing replay/restoration and ordinary eligible reversal remain available; newly forged/manual reserved-event appends reject. Root's two additional normal migration regressions and independent actual archived-M3 histories test both sides without rewriting M3 assets or granting new control-posting authority.

`procurement_reconcile` is end-of-day: immutable opening plus eligible dated stock movements, receipt allocations and supplier open items must match same-cutoff GL. Opening-only beginning-of-day reports remain unchanged. Reports return stock, received-unbilled remainders, payables and individually named controls; missing/uninitialized data is unavailable. Preview is read-only and shows exact movements, total, journal lines or explicit nonfinancial discriminator. Unposted PO rows are staged in memory, not durable editable drafts. Failed commits retain safe input, and discard/cancel/restart warnings disclose loss of unposted staging; resuming an unposted order after restart is not supported. Posted PO/source bodies are immutable and unsupported posted reversal/cancellation rejects. UX-08 remains partial for durable editable drafts.
