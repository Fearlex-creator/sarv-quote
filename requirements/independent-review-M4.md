## Design challenges required before acceptance

1. **One economic effect across retry keys.** Persistent document/source identity, command fingerprint and request alias must prevent a second receipt/bill/transfer even with another retry key, reopening, contention or a locked period. Identical completed retries can return their original result under later locks; changed content must reject unchanged. PO edits/revisions freeze at the documented receipt boundary; source identity and line remaining quantities cannot be reset by editing an order after partial receipt.
2. **Nonfinancial events remain explicit.** Saving an order does not change on-hand, value, GRNI, AP or GL. Zero-value receipts/bills still retain quantities, allocations, source and lifecycle transitions without violating ACC-02 by creating an empty financial journal. With the frozen shared Inventory1301 mapping, a warehouse transfer changes two locations at identical attributable cost and has no net GL change: use explicit movement/command/audit history rather than an empty journal, balancing plug or invented revenue/expense. A future distinct inventory-account transfer is a separate mapping scope.
3. **Cumulative rounding and conservation.** A price times selected-unit quantity is rounded once at the declared stage. Two receipts of one order line and two bills of one receipt must not each claim an independently rounded full share: retain authoritative original/cumulative allocations and final exact residual. No rounded carton→piece unit-price guess, no hidden discount or GRNI difference. Exact selected/base units, rational pack snapshots, source price, allocated cost and consumed amounts must be replayable. Zero-value positive quantity must be intentional and evidenced.
4. **Match and eligibility are per source.** Receipts cannot exceed remaining ordered base quantity; bills cannot exceed remaining eligible received/unbilled quantity/value, mix suppliers/company, consume another receipt's allocation or capitalize stock twice. A bill with cost variance must show/block the unresolved variance under v1 rather than silently rewriting stock/COGS/GRNI. Opening GRNI50 remains a separately identified outstanding source unless an explicit supported command bills it; an unsupported legacy/opening match cannot disappear from operational controls.
5. **Durable stock chronology.** The M3 policy promises nondecreasing financial dates for company stock-changing postings. Checking only one warehouse or item is insufficient: reject a backdated transfer/receipt after a later movement elsewhere in the same company. Same-day order is durable sequence, not UUID/UI order or UTC timestamp. Transfer issue cost is half-up remaining source value×quantity/source quantity; final full quantity consumes all remaining value. A transfer receives exactly that integer cost at target and conserves company quantity/value atomically.
6. **Duplicate bill review is distinct from retry.** PUR-02 considers company, supplier, invoice reference, date and amount, including safe Persian/Arabic normalization. An exact retry of an existing bill returns it; a new document identity with likely duplicate business fields must be flagged for an explicit review outcome, never silently auto-merged or auto-posted. Date/amount differences and same references for different suppliers remain distinguishable. A bounded hard block may be honest, but is not a complete duplicate-review workflow.
7. **Master data after opening.** Operational resource creation must be available without a second opening. New suppliers/units/items/warehouses need company-isolated stable IDs, exact factors, proper roles, traceable creation and archive rules. Later current-master edits/archiving cannot reinterpret historical PO/receipt/bill/transfer snapshots. Direct public/manual control or reversal APIs cannot bypass required operational effects.
8. **Opening boundary versus operational cutoff.** M3 beginning-of-day opening reconciliation remains the original1,251 fixture after operations, including operations on opening day. A distinctly labelled EOD operational report derives balances from original registers plus eligible sequenced posted events at its cutoff; it must include all original controls and expose unsupported/incomplete exceptions. Inventory pool sum must equal stock movement reconstruction and GL; unbilled receipt allocations must equal GRNI; opening AP plus new unpaid bills must equal AP. Do not present opening-only controls as current books or an unavailable operational report as verified zero.
9. **Reproducible integrity and atomic persistence.** Replay must derive each pool cost/allocation and compare stored source, policy/mapping/master snapshots, journal/marker identities, remaining quantities/values and audit. Altered stored transfer cost or source receipt value must fail validation and recomputed-envelope restore, rather than being trusted as a replay input. CAS contention, actual owned process kill before/after durable commit, failure/cancel and all validation errors must leave old or complete state. Checksums are integrity commitments, not authentication against an owner who controls every file.
10. **Schema5 and truthful native evidence.** Upgrade an actual retained schema4 snapshot containing quotations, manual journals and M3 positive/zero initialization histories, verify the original recovery before migration, preserve all prior IDs/values/output and fresh restore. Final acceptance needs frozen source/executable hashes, independently persisted arithmetic, machine-readable reconstruction and actual GTK/PDF inspection at standard/650 Light/Dark with keyboard entry, errors, partial document status and source drilldown. Headless pass counts or internal smoke hooks alone cannot certify the user workflow.
