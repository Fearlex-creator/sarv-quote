## Design challenges sent before implementation

1. **One stock authority across modules.** M4 `procurement_pools`, `procurement_stock_date` and reconciliation currently inspect procurement records alone. M5 must merge purchase, sale, transfer and return movements by durable company sequence and enforce the company-wide latest stock date in both command families. A sale followed by a purchase/transfer must neither recreate sold stock nor reject valid provenance because modules replay separately. Same-date ordering must survive restart/replay/restore and cannot depend on record UUID, vector concatenation or UI row order. Sequence ties and fabricated before/after movement pools must reject.
2. **Distinct documents, one recognition boundary.** Delivery/invoice and restock/credit retain distinct stable IDs, states and traceable line provenance even when posted in one transaction. A missing side or different financial date must fail with no partial source, journal, movement, open item, numbering, alias or audit effect. Do not silently replace policy-v1 delivery recognition with invoice-only recognition.
3. **Quote conversion snapshots.** Store original quotation ID, series, accepted version/hash and line correspondence alongside explicit catalog/core identity mappings and frozen values. Existing `QuoteLine` has no immutable ID: preserve its original accepted-line index/hash and assign immutable new order-line IDs without modifying the quote. A later quote/customer/catalog/unit edit must not modify the order or its issued documents. Tax-enabled historical quotes remain intact; conversion must reject unsupported tax, or require a separate visible newly authored tax-disabled order whose lineage and changed terms are explicit. Never strip tax or reinterpret legacy quotation units during automatic conversion. Duplicate conversion against the same accepted quote revision needs a declared identity rule, rather than new source IDs bypassing a guard.
4. **Original allocations and residuals.** The recorded integer net, line discount, document discount and issue cost are authoritative. Every component uses monotonic cumulative original-allocation credit, with the final exact residual. Derived credited gross equals credited net plus credited discounts. Returns use each particular delivered line's frozen issue cost, even after later purchases/transfers alter averages. Partial fulfillment itself also needs cumulative allocations so multiple invoices exactly conserve the order amounts.
5. **Identity and retries.** New typed natural identity is company/source/event across *all* revisions; a changed revision/content rejects under either identical or fresh keys. Financial and nonfinancial aliases share a global request namespace with manual ledger, opening, zero opening, procurement and sales. The paired typed/financial reservation may represent one identical effect, never a collision exemption for unrelated content. IDs of sales documents/lines/movements must participate in global uniqueness and procurement's known-ID checks.
6. **Actual M4 backward compatibility.** New generic writes to `sales.*` must be reserved. Actual historical M4 manual journals/aliases/reversals using that prefix must replay and restore without gaining typed stock/open-item effects. A generated future fixture is insufficient: create the legacy source with the accepted old build/tag. Existing manual revision identity remains its documented M4 behavior; do not retroactively apply new typed all-revision identity to old manual journals. Lock validation applies to all new financial/source effects and permissible aliases must remain idempotent after a later lock.
7. **Complete controls and exports.** M4 procurement reconciliation currently copies opening AR detail. New AR must include sales and original-linked credits at cutoff in both sales and procurement reports. AP/GRNI must include all M4 effects, inventory includes both modules, and COGS reconciles issue/restock costs. Unsupported controls show unavailable/error rather than verified zero. Export/backup preserves all IDs, separate documents, snapshots, component allocations, movements, requests and audit; fresh restore validates by replay and matches exact state/report semantics.
