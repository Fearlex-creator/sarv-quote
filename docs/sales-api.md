## Scope and policy
Stock goods: direct or authentic approved-quotation-linked SalesOrder -> partial paired FulfillOrder -> normal original-warehouse restock ReturnSale+credit. Delivery/invoice and return/credit have distinct stable IDs/references and linked states, but paired posting is one atomic event/date under EXISTING core-policy-v1. Quote/order/draft never posts GL/onhand. No unbilled delivery, pre-delivery invoice, payment/advance/refund/credit allocation, service sale, damage disposal, purchase return, VAT/tax submission/backdated recost in M5. Existing tax-bearing quotations remain unchanged and conversion is blocked, never silently tax-stripped. These future routes remain tracked.
